# Participant Instructions

## Before you begin

Thank you for considering this learning-tool pilot. The purpose is to evaluate the software, not
your programming ability. Participation is voluntary. You may pause, skip a question, or stop at
any time without giving a reason.

The application stores a random session ID, assigned study condition, exercise IDs, submission
outcomes, active time reported by the page, assistance use, and confidence ratings. It does not ask
for your name or email and does not store the source code you submit. The study organizer must tell
you separately whether observation notes, screen recording, or audio recording will be used.

Do not begin until the organizer has explained consent, data retention, and how to request removal
of your study data.

## During the session

1. Start the anonymous A/B session when the moderator asks.
2. Work through the exercises in the order provided by the moderator.
3. Say what you are thinking as you look for information, choose an action, or interpret feedback.
4. Use only the controls visible in your assigned condition. Some sessions intentionally provide
   staged assistance and others provide traditional debugging feedback.
5. Ask the moderator to repeat a task prompt if needed. The moderator may not explain the solution
   while a task is underway.
6. After the tasks, record your confidence rating and complete the short questionnaire.

You are not expected to finish every exercise. Errors, uncertainty, and criticism are useful
feedback about the tool.

## Avoid personal information

Do not type your name, email, passwords, private account data, school records, or other personal
information into the code editor or feedback fields. Use only the example data included with the
exercise.

## After the session

The moderator will explain the two study conditions and answer questions. Save the study contact
and your anonymous session ID only if the approved withdrawal procedure requires them. Do not post
your session ID publicly.
