# Post-Study Questionnaire

## Administration

Give this questionnaire after the assigned tasks and before discussing the difference between the
two conditions. Participation and individual questions must remain optional. Do not pre-fill
responses or create sample participants in the study dataset.

## Rating scale

For statements 1–9, use the same five-point scale:

1. Strongly disagree
2. Disagree
3. Neither agree nor disagree
4. Agree
5. Strongly agree

## Statements

1. I could find an exercise without help.
2. The exercise instructions were clear.
3. I understood how to run my code and view the results.
4. The test feedback helped me decide what to try next.
5. I understood why learning assistance was available or unavailable in my session.
6. The interface supported my learning without distracting me.
7. I felt confident making progress on the exercises.
8. I found the workflow unnecessarily frustrating. *(Reverse-coded for a combined score.)*
9. I would use this tool for another introductory Python exercise.

If staged assistance was available, also answer:

10. The hints became more specific at a useful pace.
11. The hints supported my reasoning without immediately giving away the answer.
12. The solution explanation was useful when I explicitly requested it.

Include a “Not applicable” option for conditional questions and exclude those responses from the
item average rather than converting them to a number.

## Open-response questions

1. What, if anything, made the exercises easier to understand?
2. What was the most confusing or frustrating part of the workflow?
3. How did the test feedback affect what you tried next?
4. If assistance was available, when was it most or least useful?
5. What is the single most important change you would make?

## Optional background bands

Collect these only if approved and necessary for interpreting the pilot:

- prior programming experience: none, less than 6 months, 6–24 months, more than 24 months;
- prior Python experience: none, introductory, intermediate, advanced;
- prior use of AI coding tools: never, occasionally, monthly, weekly or more.

Do not collect names, student IDs, exact ages, email addresses, or free-form demographic details in
the application database.
